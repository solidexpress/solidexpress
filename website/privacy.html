Privacy

We have no interest in your personal data.

Summary

SolidExpress is desktop CAD software. The Express Consortium does not collect, sell, or monetize personal information through the application or this website.

  • No accounts, telemetry, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers in the SolidExpress app
  • No phone-home of models, sketches, or documents
  • Documents stay on your machine (or wherever you choose to save them)
  • This marketing site is static; we do not run user tracking for marketing purposes

What we do not collect

We do not request or process names, email addresses, location, contacts, payment details, or device identifiers for product use. Opening, editing, and exporting models does not require sending data to us.

Third-party services

Downloads and issue tracking may use GitHub. GitHub’s own privacy terms apply when you visit github.com. App stores (if you install from them) have their own policies for store accounts and payments — those are not SolidExpress data collection.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/solidexpress/solidexpress/issues or use Support.
