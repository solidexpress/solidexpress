# Datum plane with offset

Goal: add reference geometry at a known offset and keep it on the timeline.

## Steps

1. **Insert → Datum Plane XY** (or XZ / YZ).
2. Enter an **Offset** (mm along the plane normal) and confirm.
3. A **Datum** feature appears on the timeline; reopen it to inspect params.

Axes and the origin point from the Insert menu are also timeline features.

Film: `datum_offset`.
