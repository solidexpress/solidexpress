# Ship hull loft

Goal: loft a small hull from two stations — a U-shaped transom and an oval bow — with clicks only.

## Steps

1. Place a **Box**. Set size **100 × 52 × 34** mm (length × beam × depth) and drop it on the origin.
2. Click the **−X** end face, **Sketch**. **Line** a closed U: narrow flat floor, vertical sides, wide capped deck (hexagon). **Exit Sketch**.
3. Click the **+X** end face, **Sketch**. **Ellipse** slightly below the face centre, smaller than the transom. **Exit Sketch**.
4. **Hide** the box so the yellow pads show.
5. Ctrl-click the transom pad, then the bow pad. **Loft** (smooth).
6. Orbit from a quarter view so the U and oval both read.

The loft reads as a hull: wide U at the stern, oval at the bow.

Film: `loft_guide_rail`.
